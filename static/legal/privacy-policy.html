Stratus Browser Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 — Effective 2026-09-19 · Applies to Stratus Browser (all release channels)

Stratus is a local-first web browser built on the open Firefox (Gecko) engine. This policy explains what information Stratus Browser collects, processes, and shares. Our default position is simple: Stratus Browser does not collect or sell your browsing data.

1. What we collect

  • Nothing by default. Stratus sends no browsing history, search history, form data, passwords, or extension activity to Stratus projects or to any third party.
  • Telemetry is disabled in Stratus builds. The Firefox telemetry system is switched off in the base configuration, and we do not operate telemetry servers to receive data from you.
  • Your profile is local. History, cookies, site data, saved passwords (optionally protected by a master password), settings, and extensions live only in your user profile on your device.

2. Network activity

  • Web pages, scripts, fonts, and other resources load directly from the sites you visit. Stratus Browser is not a proxy service; sites you visit see network traffic from your IP address exactly as with any other browser.
  • The built-in traffic overview counts requests per site, per tab, and per extension so you can see what connects where. Counting happens locally on your device and is never transmitted.
  • DNS resolution uses your operating system or configured DNS settings. A built-in custom DNS / DoH feature, when enabled, changes resolution only through your explicitly selected resolver.

3. Extensions and add-ons

Extensions you install are third-party software governed by their own privacy policies. Stratus shows you the permissions an extension requests before it can run, and the traffic overview surfaces which extensions are making network requests. Review permissions before installing extensions.

4. Updates

Stratus checks for updates so you receive security fixes. An update check contacts the Stratus update service with standard platform and version identifiers only. This can be disabled in settings at any time.

5. Your choices

  • Private windows keep session activity isolated from your normal profile.
  • Tracking protection and per-site permissions give you control over what sites can do.
  • Clear data lets you remove cookies, cache, site data, and history at any time.
  • Per-mode tools (for example a VPN or DNS resolver connected to private mode only) are never silently applied to your normal browsing.

6. Third-party sites

Sites you visit and services you log into have their own privacy policies. Stratus cannot control what websites do with the information you choose to share with them.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: stratus-browser.org/privacy.
